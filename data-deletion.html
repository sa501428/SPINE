Facebook data deletion

Delete your SPINE data

SPINE processes imported information only in your browser’s memory and does not maintain a server-side user database.

Remove the current analysis

  1. Open SPINE in the browser tab where your analysis is active.
  2. Open the ••• menu beside Export JSON.
  3. Choose Clear data.

Refreshing or closing that browser tab also removes the in-memory analysis.

Remove Facebook authorization

In Facebook, open Settings & privacy, choose Settings, then Apps and websites. Select SPINE and remove it. Facebook controls the wording and location of these settings and may change them over time.

Delete exported files

If you exported JSON or CSV data, delete those files from your device and any backup or cloud folder where you placed them. SPINE cannot access or delete files after your browser downloads them.
