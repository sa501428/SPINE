Privacy policy

SPINE is a client-side visualization tool. It turns Facebook photo tag metadata, demo data, or a local JSON file into a relationship matrix.

Information processed

When you choose Facebook import, SPINE requests your public profile and access to photo records available through Facebook’s user_photos permission. It reads photo identifiers, dates when available, and the identifiers and names of people included in accessible photo tags. Version 1.0 does not request or display image contents.

Local processing

Facebook access tokens, names, tag metadata, imported files, and relationship calculations remain in your browser’s memory. SPINE does not send this information to an application server, advertising service, or analytics service.

Storage and deletion

SPINE does not persist imported personal data. Choosing Clear Data, signing out, closing the tab, or refreshing the page removes the current in-memory analysis. Files you explicitly export are stored wherever your browser saves downloads and remain under your control.

Facebook’s role

Facebook controls which photo and tag records its API returns. SPINE cannot access records withheld by Facebook permissions, privacy settings, API limitations, or App Review status.
